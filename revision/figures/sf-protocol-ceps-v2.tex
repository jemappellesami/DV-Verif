% (page 439 x 223 bp), with the bottom equation Xbar = (1/N) sum f(x_j).
% What is drawn:
%   - a stack of cards, one per copy j <= N (double arrow, top left): a
%     blue card at the back, then three identical red cards;
%   - on each red card, left: the sampling by mu of the target index i
%     (white die) and, by D_i, of the Pauli observable P in P_1 (blue
%     die); right, three panels: receive rho (blue), measure CP_iC^dagger
%     by back-propagation (pink, dashed box), binary outcome x_j as the
%     product of the outcomes (grey);
%   - below: Xbar = (1/N) sum f(x_j) => Wbar.
% Coordinates are in bp, origin at the top-left corner, y pointing down,
% read from the submitted PDF, so the figure keeps its size and layout.
% The two dice are the bitmaps of the submitted figure (img/die-*.png);
% the pips recoloured on the white die are drawn on top, as there.
% Sans fonts, as in the submitted figure.
%

% ======================================================================
